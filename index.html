ABOUT

Gopala Krishna, Gopal to most people, is a producer, director and editor in New York, and one of the founders of Evolve Pictures. He has an MA in Media Studies from The New School.

His short film Boundaries, which he wrote, directed, produced and edited, was an Official Selection at Lighthouse International Film Festival 2025. It took a very patient cast and one spite-fuelled all-nighter.

In school he worked in the film equipment office, looking after more than $500K of gear. These days he runs productions end to end, with the full binder: call sheets, shot lists, schedules and budgets.

The part he likes most is getting a DP, an editor, a sound person and the talent pulling the same way. He takes a project from the first idea to the final export and tries to keep everyone sane in between.
