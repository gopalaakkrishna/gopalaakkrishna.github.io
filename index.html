Bio

About

Creative Producer and Director based in New York City, working across the full production pipeline from first story conversation to final export.

I’m Gopala Krishna, a Creative Producer and Director based in New York City and part of the founding team at Evolve Pictures LLC.

My short film Boundaries was written, produced, directed, and edited solo, and became an Official Selection at Lighthouse International Film Festival 2025.

I hold a Master’s in Media Studies and work across film, branded content, digital video, photography, academic writing, and production management.

My approach is simple: I take real ownership of outcomes, not just tasks, whether that means directing on location, coordinating a crew, or cutting a social reel in post.
