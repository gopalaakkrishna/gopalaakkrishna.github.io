BIO
ABOUT

I'm Gopala Krishna — a Creative Producer, Director, and Media Production Specialist based in the New York metro area, currently producing and pitching at Evolve Pictures. Over the past three years I've produced documentary films, narrative shorts, branded content, and social video campaigns end-to-end.

"I've spent my whole life obsessing over stories — how they're told, who tells them, and what it takes to bring the right people into a room and make something that actually matters. What defines how I work is this: I show up completely. Obsessive about preparation, relentless about finding the right solution, deeply collaborative by nature."

My short film "Boundaries" — written, produced, directed, and edited solo — was an Official Selection at Lighthouse International Film Festival 2025. I produced and directed "The Void", adapted with permission from author Andy Weir. I hold a Master's in Media Studies & Management from The New School (GPA 3.8).

I'm at a point in my career where I'm not just looking for a job — I'm looking for a team I can grow with, learn from, and contribute to fully. Open to full-time roles, project-based gigs, and creative partnerships.
