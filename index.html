ABOUT

I'm Gopala Krishna — filmmaker, producer, and editor. Part of the founding team at Evolve Pictures LLC. Master's in Media Studies from The New School.

My short film "Boundaries" — written, directed, produced, and edited solo — was an Official Selection at Lighthouse International Film Festival 2025. It took stubbornness, a very patient cast, and one spite-fuelled all-nighter to finish. It was worth it.

I've worked across documentary, narrative, and commercial production. I've managed $500K+ in gear, run full production binders, and delivered on 25+ projects without a single failed delivery. I show up fully and take real ownership — not just of the task, but of the outcome.

What pulls me to this work is the human orchestration of it. Connecting a cinematographer, an editor, a sound designer, and talent toward one shared vision — and building the conditions where all of it clicks together. That's the part I never get tired of.
