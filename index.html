Bio

About

Gopala Krishna — Creative Producer and Director based in New York City, part of the founding team at Evolve Pictures LLC. I own projects end-to-end: from the first story conversation to final export and publishing.


My short film "Boundaries" — written, produced, directed, and edited entirely solo — was an Official Selection at Lighthouse International Film Festival 2025. I hold a Master's in Media Studies and bring 4+ years across film, branded content, and digital video.


I thrive in environments where creative vision and strategic execution have to coexist. I show up fully and take real ownership of the outcome, not just the task.
